Title: Egypt Making Charges (Masna'eya), Stamp Fees, and Cashback Rules
Category: Egypt Gold Buying Guide
Date: 2026-11-15
Source: AurumAI Egypt Market Desk

When buying physical gold in Egypt, the total invoice price consists of three components:
Total Price = (Weight in Grams × Live Karat Price per Gram) + (Weight × Making Charge & Official Stamp Fee per Gram)

In Egypt, the fabrication and dealer fee is called **masna'eya (مصنعية)**, and the government assay/hallmark fee is called **damgha (دمغة)**. Minimizing non-recoverable masna'eya is one of the fastest ways to boost your net investment return.

**How Masna'eya Scales with Weight (Typical Accredited Brand Ranges in Egypt):**
- **1g 24K Bar:** Highest per-gram cost (typically EGP 150 to EGP 185+ per gram including stamp)—meaning a steep upfront percentage hurdle.
- **2.5g to 5g 24K Bars:** Moderate per-gram cost (typically EGP 65 to EGP 110 per gram).
- **10g to 20g 24K Bars:** Efficient sweet spot for retail savers (typically EGP 55 to EGP 70 per gram).
- **31.1g (1 oz), 50g, and 100g 24K Bars:** Lowest per-gram cost (typically EGP 45 to EGP 60 per gram).
- **Branded Sealed 21K Gold Pound (8g):** Very economical (typically EGP 50 to EGP 65 per gram, or ~EGP 400 to EGP 520 total per pound).
- **Commercial 21K/18K Jewelry:** Commonly EGP 120 to EGP 350+ per gram (and sometimes 15%–25% for designer brands), making jewelry unsuitable for pure financial investing.

**The Sealed-Packaging Cashback Rule (Critical for Egypt Buyers):**
Accredited Egyptian bullion manufacturers (such as BTC, Swiss Gold, and Selema Gold) offer a **cashback refund** (typically **35% to 50% of the paid masna'eya**, e.g., EGP 20 to EGP 30+ per gram depending on brand and weight) when you resell the bar or gold pound back to an authorized distributor—**ONLY IF the tamper-evident plastic assay card remains completely sealed and undamaged**.
- **Never open or peel the plastic packaging** of a bullion bar or branded gold pound. The moment the seal is broken, the dealer treats it as "opened/broken gold" (*kasr*), forfeits the cashback refund, and deducts full making charges.

**Practical Strategy for Small-Budget Egyptian Savers:** Rather than buying a high-masna'eya 1g bar every month, either invest monthly into a regulated Egyptian gold mutual fund (like AZ-Gold) or accumulate cash for 3–5 months in a high-yield money market fund and then buy a lower-masna'eya 5g–10g 24K bar or an 8g 21K gold pound.
