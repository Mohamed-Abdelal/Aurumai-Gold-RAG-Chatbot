Title: Egypt Gold Buying Guide: 24K Bullion Bars vs. 21K Gold Pounds
Category: Egypt Gold Buying Guide
Date: 2026-11-15
Source: AurumAI Egypt Market Desk

In Egypt, retail savers buying physical gold for investment primarily choose between two flagship instruments: **24-karat bullion bars (*sabeeka* / سبيكة)** and **21-karat Egyptian gold pounds (*gineih dahab* / جنيه ذهب)**. Understanding their exact purity, weight, making charges, and liquidity trade-offs helps Egyptian investors maximize pure gold weight per EGP spent.

**1. 24K Gold Bullion Bars (*Sabeeka*):**
- **Purity:** 999.9 fine gold (24 karats — 99.99% pure gold).
- **Standard Weights:** Available in 1g, 2.5g, 5g, 10g, 20g, 1 troy ounce (31.1g), 50g, and 100g from accredited Egyptian bullion brands such as BTC (Bullion Trading Center), Swiss Gold, Selema Gold, and SAM.
- **Making Charges (*Masna'eya*) Efficiency:** Small 1g and 2.5g bars carry relatively high per-gram making charges, whereas **10g, 20g, 31.1g, 50g, and 100g bars** offer the lowest per-gram making charges in the Egyptian market.
- **Best For:** Investors planning to invest mid-to-large amounts (buying 10g+ bars) who want maximum purity, international recognizability, and the lowest per-gram cost at higher weights.

**2. 21K Egyptian Gold Pounds (*Gineih Dahab*):**
- **Purity & Exact Weight:** Standard full gold pound weighs **8 grams of 21-karat gold** (87.5% purity), which equals exactly **7.0 grams of pure 24K gold** plus 1 gram of copper/alloy for durability. Half-pounds (4g of 21K) and quarter-pounds (2g of 21K) are also sold, though fractional pounds have slightly higher per-gram making charges.
- **Making Charges & Liquidity:** Branded, sealed 21K gold pounds (from accredited issuers like BTC or Swiss Gold) carry very competitive making charges—often lower per gram than small 1g–5g 24K bars—and enjoy universal instant liquidity at virtually every jewelry shop (*sagha*) across Egypt because Egypt's benchmark daily retail gold price is quoted in **21K per gram**.
- **Important Distinction — Branded Sealed Pound vs. Local Unbranded (*Baladi*) Pound:** Always prefer **branded, factory-sealed, officially hallmarked gold pounds** over unbranded *baladi* pounds. Branded pounds guarantee exact 21K purity, come with a tamper-evident assay card, and qualify for partial making-charge cashback upon resale, whereas unbranded *baladi* pounds are harder to verify and sometimes face steeper resale discounts.

**Head-to-Head Verdict for Egypt:**
- For budgets matching roughly 7g–8g of gold (~1 pound), a **branded sealed 21K gold pound** or a **10g 24K bar** are both top-tier, low-spread choices.
- For larger lump sums (20g, 31.1g, 50g, 100g), **24K bullion bars** win on lowest per-gram making charge and pure international bullion form.
- Avoid 18K or 21K commercial jewelry for investing, as jewelry making charges are far higher and almost 100% lost on resale.
