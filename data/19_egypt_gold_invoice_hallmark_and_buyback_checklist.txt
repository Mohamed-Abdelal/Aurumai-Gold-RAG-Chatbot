Title: Egypt Physical Gold Invoice, Hallmark, and Buyback Checklist
Category: Egypt Gold Buying Guide
Date: 2026-11-15
Source: AurumAI Egypt Market Desk

Buying physical gold safely in Egypt—and ensuring you receive top market value with cashback when you eventually sell—requires strict adherence to a pre-purchase and buyback checklist. Missing a single invoice field or damaging the seal can cost you thousands of Egyptian Pounds upon resale.

**1. Official Government Hallmark (*Maslahat Al-Damgha* / مصلحة الدمغة والموازين):**
- Every legal gold bar and gold pound sold in Egypt must be laser-hallmarked or stamped by the Egyptian Assay and Weights Administration (*Maslahat Al-Damgha*).
- Verify the karat stamp (**999.9** for 24K bullion bars; **875** for 21K gold pounds) and the accredited manufacturer's logo (e.g., BTC, Swiss Gold, Selema Gold, SAM) visible through the clear tamper-evident packaging.

**2. Mandatory Official Tax Invoice (*Fatora Darebeya*) Checklist:**
Never accept a handwritten informal receipt (*bayan as'ar*). Under Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency rules, insist on an official stamped tax invoice showing all 7 mandatory fields:
- Seller's registered commercial name, address, commercial registration number (*segel togari*), and tax card number (*betaqa darebeya*).
- Exact date of purchase and sequential invoice number.
- Item description (e.g., "BTC 10g 24K Sealed Bullion Bar" or "Swiss Gold 8g 21K Sealed Gold Pound") plus serial number if printed on the assay card.
- Exact weight in grams (verified on a calibrated digital scale in front of you before paying).
- Karat/purity (24K or 21K).
- Breakdown of raw gold price per gram AND making charge (*masna'eya*) + stamp fee (*damgha*) per gram, plus total paid in EGP.
- Explicit cashback refund terms written or stamped on the invoice/packaging, and the merchant's official stamp and signature.

**3. Safe Storage & Buyback Liquidity Checklist:**
- **Keep the Plastic Assay Pack 100% Intact:** Store bars flat in a dry pouch away from heat, keys, or bending. Torn or unsealed packs forfeit the 35%–50% *masna'eya* cashback.
- **Keep Physical + Digital Copies of the Invoice:** Photograph every invoice and bar serial number immediately and back them up securely in cloud storage, while keeping the original paper invoice paired with the gold.
- **Compare 2–3 Buyback Quotes Before Selling:** On the day you sell, check the live Egyptian 21K/24K spot buyback price across multiple reputable dealers or the brand's official distributor branches. Remember that dealers buy back at the live **bid** price (slightly below the ask price) plus the sealed-pack cashback—confirm no unauthorized hidden deductions are applied.
